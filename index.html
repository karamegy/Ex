WHO WE ARE?

We provide complete logistics solutions - from sea and air freight to land transport and customs clearance - ensuring that every shipment arrives safely, on time, and with full visibility.

With strong international partnerships and exclusive agents in Morocco, China, UAE, Turkey, and Saudi Arabia, U-Shipper offers global reach with a local touch. Our commitment to quality, reliability, and transparency drives everything we do.

OUR SERVICES

Comprehensive, reliable, and cost-effective transport solutions tailored to your business.

SEA FREIGHT

FCL and LCL worldwide services, weekly sailings, reefer containers, break bulk, project cargo, and IMO-certified hazardous cargo handling.

AIR FREIGHT

Fast, secure airport-to-airport and door-to-door delivery, charter flight arrangements, temperature-controlled and DG certified cargo handling.

LAND TRANSPORT

Domestic and international trucking across Egypt and beyond, 20ft/40ft containers, flatbed & low-bed trailers, TIR customs transit, and GPS tracking.

CUSTOMS CLEARANCE

Professional licensed brokerage at all Egyptian ports and global gateways, tariff classification, duty calculation, and free zone handling.

SPECIAL CARGO

Specialized handling, storage, and transportation for oversized equipment, hazardous materials, temperature-sensitive items, and complex project logistics.

Our Mission

To empower global trade by delivering innovative, reliable, and cost-effective logistics solutions. We are committed to providing seamless transportation, transparent communication, and personalized customer support — ensuring every shipment moves safely, on time, and with complete peace of mind.

Our Vision

To become a recognized global leader in logistics and transportation, connecting Egypt to the world through innovation, efficiency, and trust. We aim to make international trade simpler, faster, and more transparent, while strengthening Egypt's position as a regional logistics hub.
